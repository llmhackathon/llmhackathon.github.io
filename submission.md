        <p class="submission-deadline">Final submissions are due: <strong>{{ site.event.submission_deadline }}</strong></p>
        <p>That is 00:00 UTC on October 23. The deadline is the same for every team worldwide, and only projects submitted by then are eligible for judging and prizes.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="submission-card">
        <h3>New This Year: Submit on Moment</h3>
        <p>This year we're co-launching the alpha version of <strong>Moment</strong>, the Open Scientific Intelligence platform. For this event you only need Moment to submit your entry, but creating your account and forming your team early will save you time on deadline day.</p>
        <ol>
            <li><strong>Every team member signs up.</strong> You can sign in with Globus or with Auth0. We slightly prefer Globus, which supports Google login and many university credentials. If one doesn't work, try the other.</li>
            <li><strong>Everyone joins the same team</strong> on Moment.</li>
            <li><strong>One team member submits the project</strong> for the whole team.</li>
        </ol>
        <a href="https://scientificintelligence.org/onboarding?campaign=osi-hackathon-2026" class="cta-button" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sign Up on Moment</a>
    </div>

    <div class="submission-card">
        <h3>What to Submit</h3>
        <ol>
            <li><strong>A video of up to 4 minutes</strong> presenting your project. Please don't speed up your voice to fit the limit.</li>
            <li><strong>A public post with your video</strong> on any platform that doesn't require a login to view, such as YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Bluesky or Threads. A thread is fine.</li>
            <li><strong>A one-page write-up</strong>, ideally with a figure.</li>
            <li><strong>A link to your code repository.</strong></li>
            <li><strong>The platforms you used.</strong> Tick the box for each platform your team used. The sponsor prizes from Valency, Adaption and marimo require that you used their platform.</li>
        </ol>
    </div>

    <div class="submission-card">
        <h3>What We're Looking For</h3>
        <p>Models and harnesses have advanced a lot since last year, so scope ambitiously. Is a real discovery in two days now possible? Favor speed over polish and learning over perfection. These are rapidly built prototypes, and not everything has to be perfect.</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Potential for impact:</strong> Could it accelerate research, learning or applications in the physical sciences or mathematics?</li>
            <li><strong>Innovativeness:</strong> How unique is the approach? Does it stand out?</li>
            <li><strong>Relevance:</strong> How meaningfully does the project advance the physical sciences or mathematics?</li>
            <li><strong>Novelty:</strong> What new approaches or capabilities did the hackathon demonstrate?</li>
        </ul>
        <p>Looking for inspiration? Browse <a href="{{ '/submissions/' | relative_url }}">more than 120 projects from past editions</a>.</p>
    </div>

            <li>Selected teams will present at a virtual event in late November.</li>
        </ul>
